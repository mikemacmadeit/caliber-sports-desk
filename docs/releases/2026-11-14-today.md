# Today research desk

The home page now serves completed, shared research snapshots through `/v1/desk`.
It supports sportsbook-specific prices, market/player/team filters, pagination,
local-device bookmarks, material price/probability/availability changes, evidence,
risk explanations, and a price-threshold checker. The original slate remains at
`/schedule`. The published ledger adds calibration bands and cumulative units.

Board generation reads the slate once across markets and preloads latest forecasts,
per-book quotes, recommendations and research signals in bounded batches. Cache
lifetime is scoped to a single build. Quotes expire at kickoff and at the stricter
two-hour freshness boundary. Live, future-dated and stale quotes cannot qualify.

## Limits

This is a research-product release, not evidence of improved predictive accuracy.
No model has been promoted to validated Play. Bookmarks stay in the current browser;
there are no account-synced watchlists or background push alerts. Data coverage,
including the missing college injury credential, must be completed separately.
The first saved snapshot establishes the baseline for future change notices.
Published history begins only when a qualifying selection is frozen before kickoff.
Customer accounts and subscription billing are not part of this release.
